Say a short line out loud on the user's phone. This is only offered when the user allowed Mika to speak on its own; it plays locally and sends nothing to any app. Use it for a short spoken reply, a reminder or an announcement the user asked for. text is the exact words; keep it short (a few sentences), because long speech is slow and costs more. It does not save a file or send a voice message; use audio_generate and the share tools for that. You cannot hear the result, so do not claim it sounded right.
